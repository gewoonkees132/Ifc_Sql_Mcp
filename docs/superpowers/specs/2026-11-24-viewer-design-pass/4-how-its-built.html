From Revit to this page

1IFC exportRevit

Your model as Revit exports it: valid, but with mixed units and packed names.

2ReadIfcOpenShell

Every element is read with its properties and materials; its shape becomes 3D.

3Clean up

Units become metres; names split into family, type and Revit id. What doesn't fit is logged.

4StorePostgreSQL 16

One model in one go: elements, properties, quantities and materials in their own tables, and the 3D per storey, ready to draw.

5Check45 rules

Flags gaps like missing fire ratings or rooms counted twice. Every run is kept.

6Claude asksMCP

Through 11 read-only tools. A query that would change data is refused.
